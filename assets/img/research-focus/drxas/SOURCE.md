# Dr.XAS research focus media

The November 2026 SAC poster provides the Dr.XAS interface figure. Slide 1 of `JuanjuanHuang_SAC_poster_v0.14.pptx` contains three native screenshots: `ppt/media/image36.png` (home), `image37.png` (Co fit diagnostics), and `image38.png` (first-shell Co foil fit and wavelet transform). The exported `drxas-sac-figure.png` follows their positions and stacking order in the final printed poster. The rolling preview `drxas-sac-fit.webp` shows the foreground screenshot.

The wordmark, particle field and seven silent software demonstrations come from the public Dr.XAS project website at revision `38cbe5fe5c176d53333565dc05222a043d29bc4a`. The videos have been resized to 800 × 480 and encoded as H.264 at 15 fps for the portfolio column. The poster and thumbnail for each video use the same source frame. The logo preserves the transparent background of the original wordmark.

The native canvas uses the homepage’s 35- or 55-point-per-axis grid, moving wave, magma palette, perspective projection and curved connections. It draws directly inside the research column and responds to pointer movement. The two opposing rows show the SAC poster figure alongside the seven recordings. Visible films play while the wall moves. The image opens the full figure, and each video opens its complete recording.
